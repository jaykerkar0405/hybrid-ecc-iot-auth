Other inconsistencies visible in the figures:
  * Fig. 1/2 x-axis is 'Vehicles in VANET' (300-1800); Table II and the paper's scope are IoT devices (100-1000), static only (conclusion).
  * Fig. 2 shows the proposed scheme's per-authentication bit cost rising from 22.4 to 28.6 with the number of vehicles;
    a fixed two-message protocol has a fixed bit count, independent of network size.
  * Fig. 2 plots 22-41 'bits' while the text says 264 bits and Table IV says 1024 bits for the proposed scheme.
  * Fig. 1 bars for Gupta 2019 and Hassan 2018 exceed the 100% axis limit and are clipped.
